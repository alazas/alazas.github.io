Curiosity beyond boundaries.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from BRAC University, Bangladesh, with a CGPA of 3.95/4.00. My interests lie in applying computational methods to challenging problems in healthcare.

My academic research has introduced me to machine learning, medical imaging, genetic data, multimodal representation learning, and explainable AI. I am particularly interested in how heterogeneous health data can be integrated to support more meaningful analysis and clinical applications.
